# Demo video script (target 2:45, hard limit 3:00)

Record with Screen Studio. Two screens side by side: host stage (laptop) and a phone (player). Have 3 player wallets funded with devnet SOL beforehand, and the sponsor wallet holding test USDC.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Landing page, tap the sample question | "Every semester, someone promises a prize for a class quiz. Sometimes it's paid. Sometimes it's 'I'll send it later.' GeeClass makes the prize real before anyone plays." |
| 0:15–0:45 | Host page: sample notes, Write the questions, scroll the review, tap a bubble to change an answer | "A lecturer pastes their notes. AI turns them into exam-style questions, and they can edit anything." |
| 0:45–1:05 | Prize step: 10 USDC, 50/30/20, Lock and open the lobby, approve in wallet | "They lock 10 USDC. One signature moves it into a vault owned by our Solana program, not by me, not by the lecturer. The quiz's fingerprint goes onchain too, so questions can't be swapped later." |
| 1:05–1:25 | Lobby with QR; phone scans and joins; names pop in | "Students scan, connect a wallet and join. Joining writes their seat onchain, so only real players can win." |
| 1:25–2:05 | Start; voice host reads Q1; phone taps an answer; reveal with counts and standings; one more question | Let the voice host carry this part. Mention: "Faster right answers score more." |
| 2:05–2:30 | Final results, Pay winners, wallet approve, Explorer showing the transfers | "One transaction pays every winner straight from the vault and refunds anything unclaimed." |
| 2:30–2:45 | Check it yourself panel: Match | "And anyone can check it: the quiz fingerprint matches what was locked before the battle." |
| 2:45–3:00 | Back to landing | "GeeClass. Your notes, your coursemates, a real prize. Built on Solana." |

Tips
- Run one full rehearsal battle first so every wallet has its token accounts and nothing surprises you on camera.
- Keep battles to 3 questions at 15 seconds for the recording.
- Turn on ElevenLabs before recording; the voice host is the moment judges remember.
